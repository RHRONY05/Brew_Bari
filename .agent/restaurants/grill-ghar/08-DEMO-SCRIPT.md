# 08 — Grill Ghar demo script (≈ 4 minutes)

For Turjo, pitching a fast-food owner. Needs: one phone (guest), one tablet or laptop (counter, logged in as Grill Ghar counter), a printed table card for table 7 (or table 99 for rehearsals).

1. **"Scan this."** Phone scans the table card → Grill Ghar menu opens, no app, no login. Point at: the red hero offer, round category icons, **Meal deals with SAVE %**, item photos with prep time and chili level.
2. **Bangla in one tap.** Tap বাং → whole menu in Bangla. Switch back.
3. **Upsell built in.** Open **Naga Smash Burger** → tap **Meal** (price jumps, "SAVE ৳50" shown) → pick a drink → **Naga** spice → **Extra cheese** → "Add meal · ৳510". *"Every guest is offered the meal and the extras, every time — no waiter forgets."*
4. Add **Hot Wings**. Open the cart → **"Goes well with"** suggests a shake → "You save with meals ৳50". Tap **Send to kitchen**.
5. **Counter.** The tablet chimes; the order shows the table, every option and the note. Tap Accept → Preparing.
6. **Back on the phone.** The tracking sheet says **"On the grill"** with a progress bar. *"Guests stop asking 'how long?'"*
7. Counter → Ready → Served. On the phone: **Request the bill** → itemised bill with 5 % VAT. Rate 5 stars → "Review us on Google".
8. **Owner control.** On the admin (Grill Ghar theme): mark an item **sold out** → the phone shows it greyed out instantly; change a price; show the printable table QR cards.
9. Close: *"This is the Premium package. Your menu, your colours, your photos — ready in days."* Point at `/brew-bari` to show the same system in a completely different café look.

**Before every pitch:** close table 99 / table 7 in the counter, so the demo starts clean.
